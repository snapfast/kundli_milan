- **Day 51-60:** Launch a simple "Astrology Guide" or "Vedic Matchmaking FAQ" on the website to start indexing for SEO. (Leverage the existing `astrology-guide.astro` page by expanding its shareable content).

### Phase 3 (Days 61-90): Optimization, Community Push, and Conversion

- **Day 81-90:** Review all Google Analytics data. Identify the exact source of the highest-converting traffic (Threads vs. Facebook vs. Direct). Pivot 100% of energy into the winning channel for the next quarter.

## 5. KPI & METRICS TRACKING

To know if this organic campaign is succeeding, track these 4 metrics weekly:

1. **Total Profile Signups (Primary):** The raw number of new, completed profiles. Track week-over-week growth.
2. **Visitor-to-Signup Conversion Rate:** Out of 100 people who visit the site, how many complete a profile? If traffic is high but this is low (e.g., under 5%), the landing page copy needs tweaking.
3. **Source/Medium Traffic (via Analytics):** Where are the visitors coming from? We need to know if Threads or Facebook is driving the actual signups, not just vanity clicks.
4. **Threads Engagement Rate:** Measure the replies and retweets/shares on your `@rahulbaliastro` posts. High engagement usually precedes a spike in website traffic.
